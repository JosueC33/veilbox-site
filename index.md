# Veilbox

An encrypted photo and video vault for iPhone.

Your photos are encrypted on your device with AES-256. There is no account, no
sign-in, and no password reset — your vault code is the only way in, and it is
the only thing that can open your backup on another device.

**Getting your photos out is always free.** Export everything back to your
camera roll whenever you like, delete your iCloud copy and walk away, or
download on a new phone. None of that needs a subscription. Only uploading is
part of Veilbox Pro.
