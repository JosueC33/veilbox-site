The one-sentence design

Your photos are encrypted on your device, before anything is stored — anywhere.

Not "encrypted on our servers." Not "in transit." Before. The unreadable version is the only version that ever exists on disk, in iCloud, or on the wire. There is no moment where your photo travels or rests as a photo.

iCloud sync, honestly

What leaves your device — and what never does

Travels to your iCloud (encrypted transport)

  • Sealed photo envelopes — ciphertext
  • Opaque record IDs — random UUIDs
  • Timestamps & sizes — the honest metadata cost of any backup
  • Album membership & device names — sealed, unreadable to Apple and to us

Never leaves. Ever.

  • Your vault code
  • The master key, unwrapped
  • A single viewable pixel
  • Album names in the clear
  • Your location, contacts, or identity

And it goes to your private iCloud database — Veilbox has no servers. There is nothing of ours to breach, and nothing of ours to subpoena.

Honest limits

What we can't protect you from —
and say out loud

  • Someone who knows your code owns your vault. No cryptography survives a disclosed password. That's why Face ID can be required as well as the code, why a wrong code silently photographs whoever typed it, and why a second "decoy" code opens a harmless second vault.
  • The person holding your unlocked device can see your screen. No app can stop that. Face-down lock, auto-lock, and instant re-lock on leaving exist to shrink that window.
  • A forgotten code means your photos are gone. We can't reset it — that's not a support gap, it's the proof no back door exists. The optional recovery key (shown once, kept only by you) is the one rope we can offer.
  • Backup metadata exists. iCloud can see how much you store and when — never what. If even that is too much, sync stays off and everything stays local.
Any vault that promises more than this is lying to you somewhere. We'd rather be trusted for what's true.
