The hard one first

"I forgot my vault code."

There is nothing anyone can do — including us. Your code is what derives the key your photos are encrypted with, and we never receive it or anything derived from it. Without the code, the encrypted data is unreadable to everybody.

We say this loudly before you set a code, because it is the honest cost of nobody else being able to open your vault.

The one safety net is the optional Recovery Key you can generate in Settings — shown once, kept only by you.

2 / 8
Your photos are yours

Do I need Pro to get my photos back?

No. Downloading, exporting to your camera roll, and deleting your iCloud copy are all free and always will be. Only uploading is part of Pro.

IF YOU STOP PAYING

New photos stop uploading. Everything already in iCloud stays there and can still be downloaded. Nothing is deleted, nothing is held hostage.

WHAT WE REFUSE TO DO

Lock your own pictures behind a lapsed subscription. A vault that ransoms your photos back to you is not a vault.

3 / 8
Us, and Apple

Can you see my photos?

No. Photos are encrypted on your iPhone before anything is uploaded, so what reaches iCloud cannot be read without your code. Album names, dates and deletion state are encrypted too — the grouping of a vault is itself sensitive.

🔒
Encrypted before it leaves. Every time.There is no moment where a photo travels or rests readable. The full mechanism, and four ways to verify it yourself, are in the security explainer.
4 / 8
If you're forced to unlock

What is the second code?

The situation a passcode cannot help with: someone standing over you, demanding you open the app. A second code opens a separate vault with its own photos and its own encryption key.

  • It cannot reach your real vault. Different key, different storage — not a hidden door.
  • Nothing inside it shows a real vault exists. It looks exactly like the whole app.
  • You give up the second code and there is nothing left to argue about.
5 / 8
Deleting

Recently Deleted, honestly

Deleted photos stay recoverable for 30 days, then are erased for good. They still take up space on your phone until then — and you can empty the folder immediately from inside the app.

Deletion that can't be undone for a moment of panic is data loss with extra steps. Deletion that pretends to be instant is dishonest. Thirty days, stated plainly, is the trade we chose.
