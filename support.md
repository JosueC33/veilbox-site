# Veilbox Support

**Contact: [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/JosueC33/veilbox-site/issues)** — a real person reads these, and you can see the answers to everyone else's questions too.

## I forgot my vault code

There is nothing anyone can do, including us. This is not a policy we can make
an exception to — your code is what derives the key your photos are encrypted
with, and we never receive it or anything derived from it. Without the code the
encrypted data is unreadable to everybody.

We say this loudly before you set a code, because it is the honest cost of
nobody else being able to open your vault.

## Do I need Pro to get my photos back?

No. Downloading, exporting to your camera roll, and deleting your iCloud copy
are all free and always will be. Only *uploading* is part of Pro. A subscription
that ended cannot leave your own photos stranded.

## Can you see my photos?

No. Photos are encrypted on your iPhone before anything is uploaded, so what
reaches iCloud is data that cannot be read without your code. Album names,
dates and deletion state are encrypted too — the *grouping* of a vault is itself
sensitive.

## What happens if I stop paying?

New photos stop uploading. Everything already in iCloud stays there and can
still be downloaded. Nothing is deleted, and nothing is held hostage.

## What is the second code?

An optional feature for the situation a passcode cannot help with: someone
standing over you demanding you open the app. A second code opens a separate
vault with its own photos and its own encryption key. It cannot reach your real
vault, and nothing inside it shows that a real vault exists.

## Recently Deleted

Deleted photos stay recoverable for 30 days, then are erased for good. They
still take up space on your phone until then.

iPhone or iPad running iOS 16 or later; runs on Apple Silicon Macs too. iCloud backup needs an iCloud account and
Veilbox Pro.
